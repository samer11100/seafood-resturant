Our Philosophy

At the Blue Harpor, We believe great seafood begains with freshness and quality. We source the finest catch daily, working with trusted local fishermen and sustainable pathers to bring the ocean's best to your table.

Our cuisine celebrates authentic Mediterranean flavors -- simple, refined, and full of character -- paired with warm, attentive service for an exceptional dining experience form start or finish.
